Three adversarial questions, answered BEFORE the code was written
(Phase 2: per-event totals + per-class fold on the block aggregate)

Q1. What input makes this check pass while the product is broken?
  (a) A fixture in which no wait STARTS before `from`. Then clipped total ==
      unclipped total and the entire clip discipline is unexercised: deleting
      the clip, and deleting merge()'s REFUSED_CLIP pre-flight, both stay
      GREEN. PIN: the §0 counters ev_clipped_records and ev_merge_refused_clip
      must both be nonzero at exit, and §0F falsifies them by re-running the
      model over a straddle-free fixture and asserting they go to zero there.
  (b) A fixture where every event id lands in a DISTINCT class. Then the
      per-class fold is a relabelling of the per-event table and a fold that
      dropped rows would still agree. PIN: §0 class_rows_multi (classes
      holding >= 2 distinct events) must be nonzero.

Q2. What single component failing makes this hang or skip, rather than fail?
  pgwt_compute_top_events() returning zero rows -- its calloc failing, or
  every fixture record refused by a predicate. A row-by-row comparison then
  loops zero times and reports agreement. PIN: every comparison asserts
  raw.num_rows >= 3 AND raw.num_rows == aggregate rows BEFORE comparing, and
  §0 event_rows_compared must be nonzero. Second shape: if
  pgwt_block_agg_enable_events() were never called, every readout REFUSES; a
  comparator that treated a refusal as "nothing to compare" would pass. So
  the model test asserts the readout returns PGWT_BAGG_OK, and §8 asserts
  the refusal separately on a deliberately un-enabled accumulator.

Q3. What here depends on timing or ordering, and what pins it?
  No wall clock anywhere -- no sleep, no retry, no tolerance. Two ordering
  dependencies: (i) the order blocks are merged in, and (ii) hash-table slot
  order in the readout. Pinned by the documented total order
  (total_ns DESC, event_id ASC / class_index ASC) asserted directly, plus an
  all-6-permutations byte-identity check on three block aggregates. The
  latency histogram's bucket boundaries are pinned by a +-1 ns sweep around
  every edge against edges LEARNED FROM the shipping function, not copied.
