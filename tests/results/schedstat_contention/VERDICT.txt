Why this run exists: the refusal paths in tests/schedstat_lib.py detect
BLINDNESS (schedstats off, no samples, vanished pid, partial coverage). They
cannot detect a counter that is readable but STUCK — a wrong field offset or
a wrong backend enumeration would yield a plausible Sigma run_delay that is
simply wrong, no refusal would fire, and every window would report a
confident unexplained remainder. This is the only artifact that shows the
reading actually MOVES with run-queue contention.

What it establishes, and what it does not:
  - ESTABLISHES that at schedstats=0 the probe refuses (rc=2, run_delay_ms
    null) instead of reporting 0 ms, and that with schedstats=1 the same
    enumeration path reads 0.0 ms for four sleeping children and
    111,775.654 ms for sixteen spinning ones on four vCPUs — 111,776x. A
    stuck or mis-offset counter cannot produce that spread.
  - DOES NOT establish the ACCURACY of run_delay against an independent
    oracle, only its responsiveness and its sign. The rehearsal's
    decomposition remains reporting-only for that reason; no bound is
    registered on the unexplained remainder (see
    docs/DEMO_REHEARSAL_CRITERIA.md section 3).
  - Is a one-shot instrument, not a gate: it flips a GLOBAL sysctl and needs
    root plus a quiet box, so it is not wired into tests/run_all.sh. Re-run
    it by hand when schedstat_probe.py's enumeration or field parsing
    changes.
