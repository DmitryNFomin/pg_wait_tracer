screen when the removal ran. So if anything else removed it first -- the
page-side safety net (_BlinkInjector.SAFETY_MARGIN_MS, dwell + 5s), a view

run: geometry PROVEN, boundary pairs 0.0, run red, detector blamed. That is
the misattribution this whole fix exists to remove, re-entering through the
back door.
