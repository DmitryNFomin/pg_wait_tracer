ANSWER to "is the chart reused and updated, or disposed and recreated?":
DISPOSED AND RECREATED -- a different instance id after the revisit
(ec_...419 -> ec_...420), disposed in leave(), re-init'ed in renderHeatmap().
BUT every one of those calls carries mountSeq = N-1: the dispose, the init and
the setOption all complete INSIDE ViewManager.refresh()'s synchronous
view.mount() call, i.e. BEFORE _mountSeq++ sets lastMount to seq N. The blink
sweep's zero point is that seq-N event and its first frame is +200ms. So the
teardown/rebuild the brief suspects is invisible to no_blink BY CONSTRUCTION
and cannot produce a 0.0495 ratio.
(Caveat on the canvas sweep: seq advanced 4->5 during it, i.e. a live tick's
mount landed mid-sweep -- on the real gate that tick reads NOT MEASURED. The
bytes stayed identical only because the mock's payload is constant; this part
is weak evidence and the init/dispose timing above is the load-bearing result.)
