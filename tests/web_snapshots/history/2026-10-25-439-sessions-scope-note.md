## The three "Left alone" cells: attribution now verified at pixel level

Regenerating on this branch reproduced, to the same ratios, the three cells
that `history/2026-10-20-481-viewer-defects-thirteen-cells.md` recorded under

That entry attributed them circumstantially to #473-1 (N-CPUs chip moved to
the left plot edge) and #475-2 (sub-millisecond spans), while stating plainly
that "attribution was not independently verified at the pixel level the way
the 13 above were, so they are reported rather than folded in."

**This regeneration closes that open question.** Cropping and reading the
differing regions confirms both attributions directly:

- `table_queries.png`, bbox (362,141)-(404,149): the committed baseline reads
  "Backend span: **0.4 s**"; the fresh render reads "Backend span: **420.0ms**".
  That is #475-2 — `backend-span.js` dropped `fmtSpanSeconds` for `fmtMs`
  in master commit `28ecbfd6`, against the mock's `completed_duration_ms:
  420.0` (`tests/mock_server.py:428`).
- `aas_chart_overview.png` / `fidelity_sampled_shading.png`, bbox
  (77,86)-(1235,102): the red "N CPUs" chip is at the **left** plot edge in
  the fresh render and at the right in the committed baseline. The full-width,
  16px-tall bbox is the left-end arrival plus the right-end departure of one
  moving chip — it did not appear. That is #473-1, `uplot-aas.js` moving the
  chip from `left + width - w` to `ncpusLabelRect` / `box.left + pad`.

So these are known, deliberate, now-attributed drift with an identified cause
in a merged commit — **not** an unexplained regression, and not evidence that
the `snapshots` gate is broken. They stay unregenerated here for the same
reason #481 left them: a cell outside this branch's documented list gets
reported, not committed, so a real regression never rides along inside
mechanical churn. Regenerating them is now a small, well-understood piece of
work that wants its own branch and a ui-review of the three diff PNGs.

## What the pane tolerance does and does not gate

`MAX_DIFF_RATIO = 0.02` with `PIXEL_THRESHOLD = 28`
(`tests/test_web_ui_snapshots.py:177-182`) is documented as sized to catch
area/colour regressions — "a dropped series colour repaints a large area" —
with text changes explicitly outside its scope. The text-sensitive instrument
is the gallery tier at 8/0.002, and it worked: it failed 7 chip-move cells in
#481. The loose pane budget is not an accident and tightening it is the wrong
fix — `ci.yml:1042-1053` records inter-session font drift consuming up to 98%
of that budget before the container pin, and the 2026-10-20 entry records a
1px `waterfall_execution` flake under full-matrix load.

The real gap is narrower and is filed separately: the Overview and Queries
**panes have no gallery twin**, so their captions are ungated by the tier that
gates text. The fix is a gallery cell or a DOM-text assertion for those panes,
not a tighter pane ratio.
