- Purpose: let a birth cohort finalise while a slot-less child still lives, and give its later slot entry a cohort without amending an immutable certificate. INFERRED from draft:14,31-33. The owner's decision does not need it.

- Smaller rule, same counted population (INFERRED design): (a) proved never-slot at EXIT: FORK then EXIT, no UNTRACED, counts in N_k only; (b) a live no-slot proof releases FORK and holds neither frontier nor cohort; (c) slot holder never attached, or slot history unknown: today's UNTRACED [fork_ns, exit_ns) in the fork cohort, unchanged; (d) a pre-admitted slot-less child that later takes a slot and dies unattached: a hard base gap [init_ts, exit_ns) with existing gap records.

- (d) is the only case the key serves; its rate is unmeasured. Cheapest check: in the existing a2-loss50 attach-timeline records, count never-attached lifetimes with init_ts - fork_ns above 1 s. Zero means drop the key.
