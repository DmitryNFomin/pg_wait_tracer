## Measured
In Live mode every tick clears the resolved window, and until the next reply returns the header shows the plain requested range (for example "21:50:01 – 22:05:01 UTC (15.0m)") while the badge says "4.1m window" and the clip says "since capture start". The pane itself stays correct. Quiet machine: reply latency 0.04-0.12 s, the plain header is visible in 0.3-0.9% of polls. Under load: tick period about 6 s, reply latency median 0.96 s (max 1.45 s), plain header in 15.9% of polls, i.e. 0.6-1.3 s of every 6 s. The UI gate reads the notice once at the end of a wait and fails when the read lands in that interval: 5 of 6 loaded runs, 1 of 4 quiet runs. One other loaded run failed on "restarted daemon did not publish its first watermark within 15s", cause not determined.

## Questions
1. Is the flicker a product defect by the owner's goals (a header that contradicts the badge and clip for about a second in every six under load), or acceptable in-flight state? Say what you saw in the screenshots.
2. The fix, smallest first: (a) keep showing the last resolved window and its notice until the next reply replaces it (never show the unresolved requested range once a resolved one exists in Live); (b) the same plus a visible "updating" state; (c) something else. What must still be cleared immediately (a range change by the user, leaving Live, a boot change, an error reply) so that a stale resolved label is never shown for a different request? Give the invariant as one sentence a test can assert on every poll, and the unit-level and gate-level tests that go red on today's code.
3. The gate: should numeric_loss keep reading once at the timeout (it found this defect that way, by accident), or assert the invariant of question 2 on every poll of the wait? What would make the gate fail deterministically on today's code under a quiet machine too (for example a delayed reply injected in the mock-server tier)?
4. The other loaded failure ("first watermark within 15s" after a daemon restart): what single measurement decides whether that is the product (a restart under load that publishes late) or a timeout chosen for a quiet machine, and does the coverage-label unit need it settled before it merges?
