## What the diagnosis found (measurements, not instructions)

- The daemon opens a capture_loss gap when any of the 15 counter-vector components moves (src/open_snapshot.c `cov_counters`, src/counter_vector.h). The tests watch one of the 15 (`ringbuf_drops_total`).
- The withheld range runs from the floor of the last watermark at or before the gap start to the detecting tick (src/coverage.c:316-353). The tests instead require the counter to move inside the test's own window. A loss detected by the final SEAL tick withholds the window while the counter movement is read after the window's end (probe round 5).
- One reproduced withhold had only `lifecycle_init_lost_total` 0 -> 1 (a process with a 2.23 ms untraced lifetime), no ring drop (probe round 7).
- On the Ubuntu machine the same probe (20 rounds) showed no gap and no counter movement (scratchpad/u2b/measure-r13.md).
- The test deletes its trace on failure (tests/test_capture_smoke.py around :2547), so the round-13 failure's component could not be named.

## Task

1. **The daemon names its gaps (the only src/ change of this round).** An opt-in diagnostic switch, in the style of the existing test-only environment switches, makes the daemon print one machine-readable stderr line per coverage gap at the moment it records the gap. The line carries everything needed to reproduce the range the reader will withhold and to name its cause: the gap reason (DROP or COUNTERS_UNKNOWN), the gap start, the floor of the watermark the reader anchors the taint to, the detecting tick's time, and every counter-vector component that moved with its delta. With the switch off, the daemon's output and behaviour are byte-for-byte what they are at 8d85321d. No trace format change, no change to what is withheld. A host test (Mac-buildable, injected counters) pins the line for: one component, two components, COUNTERS_UNKNOWN, switch off.
2. **The witness rule, in one place (tests/coverage_loss.py), replacing the in-window counter rule.** An unmet numeric check is excused only if all of these hold; anything else is an ordinary failure:
   - every check that does not depend on withheld whole-window numbers ran and passed;
   - the check's own response is incomplete with reasons exactly `["capture_loss"]`;
   - every uncovered range in that response equals, after clipping to the request, the range that this run's logged DROP gaps produce (from the logged floor to the logged detecting tick; overlapping gaps merge). A range that no logged gap of this run's boot explains, or that extends past a gap's detecting tick, is an ordinary failure;
   - every component named by the gaps that explain the range maps to a filed defect: `event_ring_drop` (the EL8 ring-drop defect, entries `|871|`) or `lifecycle_init_drop` (the untraced-short-backend defect, entries `|959|`). Any other component, and any COUNTERS_UNKNOWN gap, is an ordinary failure;
   - cross-check against a second source: the control socket's final totals for each named component are at least the sum of the logged deltas, and the raw trace holds a GAP record for each logged gap.
   Exit code: 94 when every excused range is explained by gaps whose components are all `event_ring_drop`; 95 when any excused range needs a gap with `lifecycle_init_drop`. Print one witness line naming the ranges, the gaps and the components.
