## What was measured (not instructions)

- In Live mode every refresh tick overwrites the displayed window: `anchorLive` (web/static/app.js around line 1472; web/static/lib/state.js around 160-166) replaces from/to, clears the resolved window, and the camera is set to the full requested span; the reply then restores the resolved window. In between, the header shows a range the client never sent ("21:50:01 – 22:05:01 UTC (15.0m)" beside a "4.1m window" badge), the chart's time axis runs over the full 15 minutes with the time before capture start unmarked, and a button moves about 120 px. On a quiet machine this state lasts 0.04-0.12 s per tick; under load 0.6-1.3 s of every 6 s.
- The UI gate (tests/ui_live_smoke.py, `numeric_loss` around lines 867-870) reads the notice once at the end of a wait and failed when that read fell in the interval: the operand record shows it in 3 loaded runs, and 3 more runs (2 loaded, 1 quiet) show a plain notice in their failure snapshot. The view's own Live/clip/mount guard did not fail in any of 10 runs.
- One loaded run failed instead on "restarted daemon did not publish its first watermark within 15s" (tests/ui_live_smoke.py around 978-995: the loop needs a status answer within 2 s, a new boot id, and watermark_wall above boot_start_wall, swallows four exception classes, and names only the third condition). Which condition never held was not recorded. Quiet runs reached that step 3 times and passed 3.
- The info reply's `gaps` and `holes` lists (src/server.c, `cov_info_lists`, introduced on this branch) disagree with the window verdicts in three synthetic cases (a loss floor before the first retained record: 5 s and 400 s overhang; an earlier boot's open loss listed across the latest boot's complete window). Nothing in web/static, src/replay.c or scripts/ reads those lists.
